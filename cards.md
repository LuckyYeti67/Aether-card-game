## Card Template

Here's the standard format for card designs:

### Card Name
- **Card Type**: [Monster/Spell/Item/Mutation/Character]
- **Aether**: [Color/Type]
- **AP Cost**: [Number]
- **Stats**: 
  - Attack: [Number] (Monsters only)
  - Defence: [Number] (Monsters only)
  - Health: [Number] (Character only)
- **Description**: [Card effect text]
- **Keywords**: [List any relevant keywords]
