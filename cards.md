### Master of Magics
- **Card Type**: Monster
- **Aether**: Water (Blue)
- **AP Cost**: 6
- **Stats**:
  - Attack: 3000
  - Defence: 4000
- **Description**: 
  - **Effect 1**: On Attack: Discard 1, Draw 1
  - **Effect 2**: HP 3: When you discard a card(s), Bounce a monster with AP cost of 3 or less
