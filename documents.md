You will have a deck consisting of 50 cards. At the start of the game each player will draw cards and charge cards from the top of thier deck based on their character card and can choose to Mulligan by putting any number of cards they drew on to the bottom of the deck then drawing back up to thier hand size and they must keep this new hand.
 After you choose to keep your hand you will then take the top 6 cards of your deck and put them into your HP Zone. If you run out of HP then you lose the game

To decide who goes first the player will be chosen randomly with a dice roll or a coin flip.

When you start your turn you will untap all your tapped cards. This is the Untap phase. you will draw 1 card from the top of your deck,then you may place a card from your hand into your AP Zone. This is called the charge phase

After your Charge Phase. You may now use your AP. AP is used to draw cards,charge a card,play a card,set a card, activate cards on the opponent's turn. and some effects will use up AP as well.

You may use your AP to draw cards and even add additional AP toy your  AP Zone.
